Built for control

Trading intelligence, not blind copying.

The system is designed to filter activity first, make each decision observable, and preserve strict position controls as it monitors selected wallets.

01

Wallet Quality Engine

Ranks wallets with 90-day activity, realized PnL, trade count, win rate, category performance, and volume thresholds.

02

MCP-Native Agents

Local MCP servers expose Polymarket and Telegram capabilities to compatible agent workflows and Claude Code sessions.

03

Telegram Command Center

Follow wallets, inspect positions, review PnL, and control notifications from a concise mobile interface.

04

Risk Guardrails

Maximum concurrent positions, fixed sizing, market-liquidity checks, duplicate-trade protection, and configurable sell behavior.

05

Full Audit Trail

PostgreSQL records wallet metadata, trade events, copy decisions, position status, PnL, and alert preferences.

06

Production Observability

Prometheus metrics and Grafana dashboards track execution latency, portfolio exposure, system health, and performance.

System flow

From public wallet data to a governed action.

01 / DISCOVER

Scan wallets

Ingest leaderboard and trade data, then score eligible wallets against your performance thresholds.

02 / FILTER

Validate signals

Reject duplicates, stale activity, low-liquidity markets, and trades that violate exposure rules.

03 / NOTIFY

Alert in Telegram

Send clear, timestamped signal information to your Telegram command center.

04 / MEASURE

Track results

Persist execution outcomes and surface PnL, latency, and health data through your dashboard.
